# Governance

The documents in this directory outline the governance principles that support the development of SPI (Stewardship Performance Index), TEEHC (The Ecological Equivalent of Hyperscale Computing), and related stewardship initiatives developed by Misfit Genius Inc.

These documents are intended to promote transparency, responsible collaboration, and clear expectations as the projects evolve through partnerships with municipalities, governments, academic institutions, industry, Indigenous communities, and other organizations.

They are not legal agreements. Instead, they describe the principles that guide future collaboration and provide a foundation for more formal agreements where appropriate.

Topics within this directory may include:

* Intellectual property principles
* Collaboration frameworks
* Licensing philosophy
* Pilot project governance
* Research partnerships
* Stewardship principles
* Future legal and organizational structures

As these projects mature, additional governance documents may be added to reflect new partnerships, lessons learned, and evolving best practices.

The objective is simple: encourage open collaboration while ensuring that stewardship, transparency, and intellectual property are respected by all participants.
