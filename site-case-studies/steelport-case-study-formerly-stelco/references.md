## Notes on Sources

This case study relies on a combination of:

* Primary planning documents
* Official project materials
* Municipal planning documents
* Government reports
* Environmental restoration documentation
* Public infrastructure studies
* Industry reporting

Where available, primary planning documents and official project materials were prioritized over media reporting.

Key sources include the Steelport Urban Design Report, Draft Plan of Subdivision, municipal planning documents, government reports, environmental restoration documentation, infrastructure studies, and official project materials.

Media coverage was used primarily to supplement publicly available planning information and to provide additional context regarding project milestones and public statements.

Economic projections, employment forecasts, and future development outcomes are presented as publicly stated projections rather than verified results.

Concept renderings, vision documents, and planning frameworks are treated separately from approved infrastructure and completed development.

Observations and emerging themes represent interpretations of the available information and should not be considered evidence of future outcomes.
