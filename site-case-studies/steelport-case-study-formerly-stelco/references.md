## Notes on Sources

This case study relies on a combination of:

* Official project materials
* Municipal planning documents
* Government reports
* Environmental restoration documentation
* Public infrastructure studies
* Industry reporting

Where possible, primary sources were prioritized over media summaries.

Economic projections, employment forecasts, and future development outcomes are presented as publicly stated projections rather than verified results.

Observations and emerging themes represent interpretations of the available information and should not be considered evidence of future outcomes.
